Our Mission

Six Pillars of
Safe AGI Development

Comprehensive frameworks across research, law, and ethics — designed to guide humanity through the most pivotal technological transition in history.

🔬

Unified Standards

Establish international standards to measure and evaluate Artificial General Intelligence with complete transparency and scientific rigor.

🛡️

Humanity Protection

Develop legal and ethical frameworks to protect humanity from the risks of advanced artificial intelligence before they become irreversible.

🌍

Global Collaboration

Unite the world's brightest minds from all nations to work in harmony on this critical civilizational challenge — no borders, no exclusions.

📊

Radical Transparency

Publish all research and standards openly so the entire world can benefit from our collective knowledge and scrutinize our work.

⚖️

Equity & Justice

Ensure fair distribution of AGI benefits across all nations and peoples without discrimination, preventing technological colonialism.

🚀

Responsible Innovation

Encourage technological innovation while ensuring ethical responsibility and social accountability at every step of development.

Why AGI Standard?

The Stakes Have Never Been Higher

Artificial General Intelligence is not merely a technological advancement — it represents a pivotal turning point in human civilization. Without unified standards and ethical frameworks, we risk genuine threats to global security, justice, and human dignity.

  • Unified definition of Artificial General Intelligence
  • Safe and transparent evaluation standards
  • International legal framework for AGI governance
  • Oversight and monitoring mechanisms
  • Protection of fundamental human rights
