Why AGI Standard

The Stakes Have Never Been Higher

Artificial General Intelligence isn't a distant scenario — it's an imminent civilizational turning point. Without unified standards and robust ethical frameworks, we face genuine threats to global security, justice, and human dignity.

AGI Standard is the global answer: bringing together the world's finest scientific, legal, and ethical minds to build the frameworks that will define humanity's future.

  • Unified, internationally recognized AGI definitions
  • Safe, transparent evaluation and certification standards
  • Comprehensive international legal framework
  • Independent oversight and continuous monitoring
  • Universal protection of human rights and dignity

Our Pillars

Built on Six Foundations

Every standard we develop rests on these core principles, ensuring our work remains aligned with humanity's long-term interests.

🔬

Unified Standards

Establish international benchmarks to measure and evaluate Artificial General Intelligence with complete scientific transparency.

🛡️

Humanity Protection

Develop robust legal and ethical frameworks that safeguard humanity from the risks of advanced artificial intelligence systems.

🌍

Global Collaboration

Unite the world's brightest minds across all nations, disciplines, and cultures to work in harmony on this critical challenge.

📊

Open Transparency

Publish all research and standards openly so the entire world can access, scrutinize, and build upon our collective knowledge.

⚖️

Equity & Justice

Ensure equitable distribution of AGI benefits across all nations and peoples, eliminating systemic disparities and discrimination.

🚀

Responsible Innovation

Champion technological progress while maintaining an unwavering commitment to ethical responsibility and social accountability.
