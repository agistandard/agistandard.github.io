Why AGI Standard?

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is not merely a technological advancement—it represents a pivotal turning point in human civilization. Without unified standards and ethical frameworks, we risk facing genuine threats to global security, justice, and human dignity.

AGI Standard is the solution: a global platform bringing together the world's finest scientific, legal, and ethical minds to establish unified standards that protect humanity while enabling responsible innovation.

  • Unified definition of Artificial General Intelligence
  • Safe and transparent evaluation standards
  • International legal framework
  • Oversight and monitoring mechanisms
  • Protection of human rights
