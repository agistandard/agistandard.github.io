The Case for Standards

Why AGI Standard?

AGI is not merely a technological advancement — it is a civilizational inflection point. Without unified governance, we risk fragmented, unsafe development that threatens global security and human dignity.

  • ✓
    Unified Definition of AGI Establishing a scientifically rigorous, internationally agreed-upon baseline for what constitutes true AGI.
  • ✓
    Safe Evaluation Protocols Transparent, reproducible benchmarks and red-team evaluation frameworks applied uniformly across all systems.
  • ✓
    International Legal Framework Treaty-level governance mechanisms with teeth — enforceable standards backed by international law.
  • ✓
    Oversight & Monitoring Continuous, independent monitoring of AGI development activities globally through an open reporting architecture.
  • ✓
    Human Rights Protection Embedding fundamental rights into every layer of AGI governance — by design, not as an afterthought.

"We cannot leave humanity's future to chance or to the unchecked ambition of any single corporation or nation. Unified, transparent standards are not optional — they are a civilizational imperative."
