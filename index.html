Why AGI Standard

The Stakes Have
Never Been Higher

Artificial General Intelligence isn't a distant scenario — it's an imminent civilizational turning point. Without unified standards and robust ethical frameworks, we face genuine threats to global security, justice, and human dignity.

AGI Standard is the global answer: bringing together the world's finest scientific, legal, and ethical minds to build the frameworks that will define humanity's future.

  • Unified, internationally recognized AGI definitions and benchmarks
  • Safe, transparent evaluation and certification standards
  • Comprehensive international legal and ethical framework
  • Independent oversight and continuous monitoring
  • Universal protection of human rights and dignity

How We Work

Our Process

A rigorous, transparent methodology that ensures every standard we publish has the highest scientific and ethical integrity.

01
RESEARCH

Global Expert Consultation

We convene researchers, policymakers, and ethicists from all 180+ member nations to identify emerging AGI risks and opportunities.

02
DRAFTING

Standard Development

Multi-disciplinary working groups draft proposed standards through iterative review cycles, ensuring scientific rigor and practical applicability.

03
REVIEW

Open Public Comment Period

All draft standards are published openly for a 90-day public comment period, inviting feedback from civil society and independent researchers.

04
RATIFICATION

International Adoption

Finalized standards are submitted to member nations for formal adoption and integrated into national AI governance frameworks.
