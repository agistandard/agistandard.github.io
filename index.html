Core Mission

Six Pillars of
Global AGI Governance

Our work is organized around six interconnected pillars, each critical to ensuring AGI development benefits all of humanity.

01
🔬

Unified Standards

Establish internationally recognized metrics and benchmarks to evaluate AGI capabilities with complete scientific rigor and transparency.

→
02
🛡️

Humanity Protection

Develop robust legal and ethical frameworks to safeguard human rights, dignity, and security from potential AGI-related threats.

→
03
🌍

Global Collaboration

Unite the world's leading researchers and institutions across all nations to address this civilizational challenge collectively.

→
04
📡

Radical Transparency

All research, standards, and findings published openly so every nation and community can access and contribute to our knowledge base.

→
05
⚖️

Equity & Justice

Guarantee fair and equitable distribution of AGI benefits across all nations and peoples, preventing concentration of transformative power.

→
06
🚀

Responsible Innovation

Foster technological progress that is matched by ethical responsibility, ensuring innovation serves humanity rather than endangering it.

Why AGI Standard?

The Urgency Is Real.
The Window Is Narrow.

Artificial General Intelligence is not merely a technological milestone — it represents a fundamental inflection point in human civilization. Without unified global standards, we face unprecedented risks.

✓
Unified definition and taxonomy of Artificial General Intelligence
✓
Safe and independently verifiable evaluation standards
✓
Binding international legal framework with enforcement mechanisms
✓
Continuous oversight, monitoring, and real-time risk assessment
✓
Guaranteed protection of fundamental human rights in the AGI era
"We cannot leave humanity's future to chance.
We need unified standards — and we need them now."

— AGI Standard Founding Charter · 2026

Roadmap

Our Path Forward

Q2 2026 — NOW
Foundation & Coalition Building
Assembling founding members, establishing governance structure, and defining the initial research agenda across six working groups.
Q4 2026
First AGI Definition Framework
Publishing the inaugural consensus document: a scientifically grounded, internationally reviewed definition of Artificial General Intelligence.
2027
Evaluation Standards Release
Open-source benchmark suite for AGI capability assessment, alongside peer-reviewed safety evaluation protocols.
2028
International Treaty Proposal
Submitting a proposed international governance treaty to the United Nations and regional bodies for ratification.
