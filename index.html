Our Mission

Six Pillars of AGI Governance

A comprehensive framework ensuring Artificial General Intelligence develops safely, equitably, and transparently for all humanity.

01
🔬

Unified Standards

Establish international benchmarks to measure and evaluate Artificial General Intelligence with complete transparency and scientific rigor.

02
🛡️

Humanity Protection

Develop robust legal and ethical frameworks to protect humanity from the risks of advanced artificial intelligence systems.

03
🌍

Global Collaboration

Unite the world's brightest minds from every nation to work in harmony on this critical civilizational challenge.

04
📊

Full Transparency

Publish all research and standards openly so every nation and institution can benefit from our collective knowledge.

05
⚖️

Equity & Justice

Ensure fair distribution of AGI benefits across all nations and peoples without discrimination or geopolitical bias.

06
🚀

Responsible Innovation

Encourage technological advancement while ensuring ethical responsibility and social accountability at every step.

Why AGI Standard

The Stakes Have
Never Been Higher

Artificial General Intelligence is not merely a technological advancement — it represents a pivotal turning point in human civilization. Without unified standards and ethical frameworks, we risk genuine threats to global security, justice, and human dignity.

AGI Standard is the solution: a global platform bringing together the finest scientific, legal, and ethical minds to establish standards that protect humanity while enabling responsible innovation.

  • ✓ Unified definition of Artificial General Intelligence
  • ✓ Safe and transparent evaluation standards
  • ✓ International legal framework
  • ✓ Oversight and monitoring mechanisms
  • ✓ Protection of universal human rights
